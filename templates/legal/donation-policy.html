Donation Policy

Donations made to SWARGIYA AMDAR SHRI SHARAD RANPISE FOUNDATION are voluntary contributions directly intended to support our grassroots programmes in educational technology, women economic empowerment, healthcare, and handicapped welfare.

As a registered non-profit organization, eligible donations made by Indian donors qualify for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, as per applicable government guidelines.

To receive an official tax-exemption receipt, donors must mandatorily provide their Permanent Account Number (PAN) and correct contact details during the donation process.

Official electronic receipts and acknowledgments are issued to donors upon successful credit verification for statutory tax compliance and transparency.
