Privacy Policy

At SWARGIYA AMDAR SHRI SHARAD RANPISE FOUNDATION, we are committed to protecting the privacy and personal security of all our donors, supporters, and website visitors. We collect basic details like name, contact info, and PAN strictly for managing inquiries and issuing official tax exemption receipts.

As a registered non-profit organization, collecting donor details is required by the Income Tax Department to issue valid tax-exemption receipts. All collected information is stored securely in our internal records and used solely for statutory updates and donor communications.

We enforce strict security measures and will never sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. All online donations are processed through bank-grade encrypted payment gateways to ensure maximum financial safety.

You retain full rights to request updates or deletion of your personal details within legal regulatory boundaries at any time. For any privacy queries or data updates, please contact our support team directly at info@sharadranpisefoundation.in.
