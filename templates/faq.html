FAQ

Answers to common questions

We believe clarity and trust are essential when supporters, volunteers, and communities connect with our mission.

Our core initiatives are dedicated to Educational Technology development, Women Economic Empowerment through vocational training, comprehensive Healthcare support, and Handicapped Welfare programs.

Partners, corporate entities, and social workers can collaborate with us as institutional partners, volunteers, or sponsors to drive large-scale grassroots community reforms.

Every contribution directly supports our on-ground execution, skill training workshops, medical assistance camps, and educational development tools managed transparently by the foundation.

The foundation is guided and directed by Mr. Yash Jayant Sarvade and Ms. Priyanka Ranpise, ensuring complete institutional integrity, transparency, and sustainable community growth.

You can connect with us directly through the contact page on our website or write to our support team for any program inquiries, volunteering opportunities, or assistance.
