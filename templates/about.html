About Us

Building communities through action and dignity

SWARGIYA AMDAR SHRI SHARAD RANPISE FOUNDATION works directly with communities to drive impactful change in Educational Technology, Women Economic Empowerment, Healthcare, and Social Welfare.

Who We Are

About Sharad Ranpise Foundation

SWARGIYA AMDAR SHRI SHARAD RANPISE FOUNDATION is dedicated to fostering grassroots development. We focus on educational technology products, women economic empowerment through vocational training, comprehensive healthcare, and handicapped welfare.

Our Vision & Mission

Creating lasting social impact

Our mission is to enable access to modern educational tech, women skill-building, healthcare support, and social welfare for communities through collaborative grassroots action.

Our Approach

Grounded in local realities

We combine grassroots listening with practical frameworks to design impactful programs across technology, education, health, and social rehabilitation.

Leadership

Driven by dedicated visionaries

Led by our directors, Mr. Yash Jayant Sarvade and Ms. Priyanka Ranpise, the foundation is committed to institutional integrity, transparency, and driving sustainable community growth across all sectors.
