About Us

Creating Change. Empowering Communities.

SWARGIYA AMDAR SHRI SHARAD RANPISE FOUNDATION is dedicated to driving grassroots change across Educational Technology, Women Economic Empowerment, Comprehensive Healthcare, and Social Welfare. We work directly with local communities to implement impactful programs and events that create long-term social growth.

Lead by experienced visionaries Mr. Yash Jayant Sarvade and Ms. Priyanka Ranpise, Sharad Ranpise Foundation is committed to fostering equitable community growth, protecting fundamental rights, and driving sustainable grassroots development across all sectors.
